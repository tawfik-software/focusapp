Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I restore my premium subscription?

If you've purchased premium on another device or reinstalled the app, go to Profile → tap "Restore Purchases" button. Your subscription will be restored automatically.

❓ How do I change the app language?

Go to the Profile screen and tap on the language selector. Focus App supports English, French, Spanish, Arabic, and Italian.

❓ How do I cancel my subscription?

For iOS: Go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Focus App → Cancel Subscription.
For Android: Open Google Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → Focus App → Cancel Subscription.

❓ Can I use Focus App offline?

Yes! Once you've downloaded the app and unlocked premium features, you can use Focus App completely offline. All your data is stored locally on your device.

❓ What's included in the premium version?

Premium unlocks:
• Access to all 11 ambient music tracks
• Detailed analytics and statistics
• Progress tracking
• Ad-free experience

❓ Is my data private?

Yes! Your focus sessions and personal data are stored locally on your device. We only collect minimal data for subscription management through RevenueCat. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

❓ The timer isn't working properly

Try these steps:
1. Make sure you've completed the initial profile setup
2. Check that your device isn't in power-saving mode
3. Restart the app
4. If the issue persists, contact support with your device model and OS version.

❓ How do I delete my data?

Go to Profile → Settings. You can clear your analytics history or uninstall the app to remove all local data. Since we don't store data on servers, uninstalling will permanently delete everything.
