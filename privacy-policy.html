Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ultra Focus Flow Study Timer ("App", "we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • User Name: When you create a profile, you may provide a display name.
  • Focus Sessions: We locally store your focus session data (duration, date, time).

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: App features used, session duration, app crashes and diagnostics.
  • Subscription Data: Purchase history, subscription status (processed by RevenueCat and Apple).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • To manage your focus sessions and statistics
  • To process and manage your subscription
  • To improve and optimize the App experience
  • To provide customer support
  • To send you updates and notifications (with your consent)

4. Data Storage

4.1 Local Storage

Your focus session data and user preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

4.2 Cloud Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect and process your data:

  • RevenueCat: Manages in-app subscriptions and purchases. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: Processes payments and manages subscriptions. See Apple Privacy Policy

5. Subscription Information

5.1 Payment Processing

All payments are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not collect or store your payment information (credit card, billing address, etc.).

5.2 Subscription Data

RevenueCat collects and processes:

  • Subscription status and tier
  • Purchase dates and renewal dates
  • Anonymous user identifier
  • Device and app version information

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • With Service Providers: RevenueCat and Apple, as necessary to provide subscription services.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, sale, or acquisition (users will be notified).

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Deletion

You can:

  • View your focus session history within the App
  • Delete your local data by uninstalling the App
  • Request deletion of your subscription data by contacting us

7.2 Subscription Management

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple ID settings:

  • Open Settings on your iOS device
  • Tap your name → Subscriptions
  • Select Ultra Focus Flow Study Timer
  • Tap Cancel Subscription

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data:

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
